KEY DELIVERABLES
================================================================================

1. VISIT TRACKING & MARKING
   - Coordinators can set visit dates and reset appointments to PROPOSED
   - Once past date/time, coordinators can mark visits as Attended or Missed
   - Only Attended visits count toward compensation (Business Rule BR-13)

2. COMPENSATION CALCULATION & PAYOUTS
   - earned_amount() = compensation × attended_visits / total_visits
   - amount_due() = earned - paid - requested (never negative)
   - Coordinators can request payouts for any amount due
   - Payout tracking with status (REQUESTED/PAID) and reference numbers

3. RECRUITMENT INSIGHTS
   - Funnel visualization: applied → pre-screen → shortlist → screened → passed → enrolled → completed
   - Screening pass rates by ML-2 band (high, medium, low, unscored)
   - Band distribution (count of applications by band)
   - Demographics (age bands, sex, region) for applied vs enrolled
   - Only participants with insights consent included (BR-17)
   - Groups 1-4 shown as "fewer than 5", 0 as "0" (BR-16)

4. COORDINATOR DASHBOARD
   - 4 metric tiles: active studies, new applications, screenings this week, payouts awaiting
   - 2 tables: applications under review, screenings in next 7 days
   - All data scoped to coordinator's organisation

5. PARTICIPATION MANAGEMENT
   - List enrolled/completed participants with attendance and payout tracking
   - Detail page for editing visit dates, marking attendance, requesting payouts
   - Withdrawal confirmation page for removing participants
   - Complete participation action to mark study as finished
